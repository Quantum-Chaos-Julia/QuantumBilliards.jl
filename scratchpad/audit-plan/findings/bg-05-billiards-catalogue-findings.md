## 2. Missing wiring
- `AnnularBilliard` registers `register_symmetries()` (trivial) even though its two concentric circles are genuinely reflection-symmetric across both axes — a strict subgroup (identity-only) of its true symmetry. **Confirmed still open**, matches bg-02's note that it "doesn't yet exercise" multi-ring folding (Gap 2).
- `TriangleBilliard` always registers `register_symmetries()` (trivial), even for the isosceles special case (`chi==1`, i.e. `alpha==beta`), which has a genuine mirror symmetry about the bisector from the `gamma` corner. New finding, low severity (depends on caller-chosen `chi`, not statically detectable, and runtime-verified below to still close correctly since no folding is attempted).
- `SymmetryWall.sector_id` ([boundarytypes.jl](/home/clozej/.julia/dev/BilliardGeometry.jl/src/geometry/boundarytypes.jl#L28)) is **never read anywhere** in either `BilliardGeometry.jl` or `QuantumBilliards.jl` (grep-confirmed). Concretely, [stadium.jl](/home/clozej/.julia/dev/BilliardGeometry.jl/src/geometry/billiards/stadium.jl) tags its D2 walls `SymmetryWall(1,4)`/`SymmetryWall(3,4)` while [circle.jl](/home/clozej/.julia/dev/BilliardGeometry.jl/src/geometry/billiards/circle.jl), [rectangle.jl](/home/clozej/.julia/dev/BilliardGeometry.jl/src/geometry/billiards/rectangle.jl), [prosen.jl](/home/clozej/.julia/dev/BilliardGeometry.jl/src/geometry/billiards/prosen.jl) use `sector_id=2` for structurally identical wall roles — this inconsistency is currently invisible because nothing consumes the field. This is concrete new evidence for bg-02's tracked **Gap 6 (confirmed still open)**.

## 3. Missing implementations
- **`LimaconBilliard`'s `full_boundary` cannot be called at all** — no `_apply_symmetry_to_curve` method exists for `LimaconSegment` in [fullboundary.jl](/home/clozej/.julia/dev/BilliardGeometry.jl/src/geometry/fullboundary.jl) (only `LineSegment`/`CircleSegment`/`FourierCoeffPolarSegment`/`PolarSegment` have one). Runtime-confirmed `MethodError` (see §8 table). **New finding**, not covered by bg-02/bg-03's summaries — a distinct gap from the already-tracked rotation/`apply_symmetry_pb` issue.
- **`MushroomBilliard`'s `full_boundary` produces a topologically wrong (non-closed) result**, even at the default, correctly-centered construction. Root cause: `full_boundary`'s generic algorithm (`P ++ reverse(image(P))`, [fullboundary.jl:169-178](/home/clozej/.julia/dev/BilliardGeometry.jl/src/geometry/fullboundary.jl#L169-L178)) implicitly assumes the fundamental domain's physical curves (`P`) form a **single** connected chain — true for every other billiard, but false for `MushroomBilliard`: its `triangle_dom` subdomain contributes **zero** physical (`SpecularReflection`) curves (all three of its edges are `SymmetryWall`/`Transparent`), so `P` is genuinely two disjoint chains (`{x_seg,circle}` and `{stem edges}`). Appending "all originals then all reflected images" instead of properly interleaving the two chains with their mirror images breaks the loop (runtime-confirmed 2.0-unit endpoint gap, not floating-point noise). **New finding**, a real algorithmic limitation in `full_boundary` itself, not merely a mushroom.jl bug.

## 4. Unstable APIs (constructor signature consistency)
Comparing all 13 constructors, several inconsistencies exist:
- Type-parameterization discipline: `AnnularBilliard`/`C3Billiard`/`CircleBilliard`/`EllipseBilliard`/`ProsenBilliard`/`RectangleBilliard`/`StarBilliard`/`PolygonBilliard` all declare `where T<:Real` on typed args; `StadiumBilliard(half_width)`, `MushroomBilliard(half_width,...)`, `LimaconBilliard(a)`, `TriangleBilliard(gamma,chi;...)` have **no `<:Real` constraint** and derive `T` via `typeof(...)`, so e.g. `StadiumBilliard(1)` (an `Int`) would silently build an `Int`-typed billiard, breaking downstream trig/sqrt.
- `center`/`origin` convention: `SVector{2,T}(zero(T),zero(T))` default in most billiards vs. plain `Vector` literals `[0.0,0.0]` in [mushroom.jl](/home/clozej/.julia/dev/BilliardGeometry.jl/src/geometry/billiards/mushroom.jl#L6) (`origin=[0.0,0.0]`) and [polar.jl](/home/clozej/.julia/dev/BilliardGeometry.jl/src/geometry/billiards/polar.jl#L14) (`center=[0.0,0.0]`) — a type-stability inconsistency, not just cosmetic.
- [mushroom.jl:12](/home/clozej/.julia/dev/BilliardGeometry.jl/src/geometry/billiards/mushroom.jl#L12) hardcodes `circle_dom = SimpleDomain{Float64}(...)`, ignoring the actual promoted type of `half_width`/`R`/`origin` — a genuine type-instability bug (new finding) distinct from anything in bg-01/bg-03/bg-04's summaries.
- `D2`-symmetric billiards (`center==(0,0)` guarded: `CircleBilliard`/`EllipseBilliard`/`RectangleBilliard`/`ProsenBilliard`) vs. `StarBilliard`, which accepts a `center` kwarg with **no equivalent guard** despite its `NFoldRotation` symmetry only being valid about the true origin — see §5/§8, runtime-confirmed to break.

## 5. Vulnerabilities (unchecked degenerate parameters)
- **`StarBilliard(center≠(0,0))`**: no `ArgumentError` guard (unlike Circle/Ellipse/Rectangle/Prosen), even though `NFoldRotation` always rotates about the true coordinate origin, not `center`. **Runtime-confirmed** to silently produce a broken, non-closed `full_boundary` (see §8 table).
- **`MushroomBilliard(origin≠[0,0])`**: no guard; several internal coordinates (`[cx,R]`, `[half_width,cy]`) mix `cx` into x-coordinates without applying `cy`/offset consistently to the corresponding y-coordinates. **Runtime-confirmed** to silently produce an even-more-broken (non-closed *and* non-simple, winding≈0) boundary instead of erroring.
- `LimaconBilliard(a)`: no validation that `a` keeps the limaçon simple (non-self-intersecting); large `|a|` produces a self-intersecting curve silently (moot right now since `full_boundary` already crashes for any `a`, but worth fixing alongside item 3's fix).
- `TriangleBilliard(gamma, chi; ...)`: no validation that the derived `alpha`/`beta` angles stay positive/sum correctly; a bad `(gamma,chi)` pair can silently build a degenerate/invalid triangle.
- `C3Billiard(a; scale=...)`: no check that `a` keeps `r(phi)>0` everywhere (self-intersection risk for large `|a|`), unlike `StarBilliard`'s explicit `R>abs(a)` check.

## 6. Consolidation opportunities
- The four "quadrant, `FourierCoeffPolarSegment`/`PolarSegment`-based, D2-symmetric" billiards (`CircleBilliard`, `EllipseBilliard`, `ProsenBilliard`, and structurally `RectangleBilliard`) repeat near-identical boilerplate: `center==(0,0)` guard, `D2_symmetry()` call, `p0`/`p1` wall construction with `SymmetryWall(1,2)`/`SymmetryWall(3,2)`. A shared internal helper (e.g. `_quadrant_symmetry_walls(p0, p1, center)` returning `(ywall, xwall)`) would remove real, now-provable duplication across 4 files.
- `C3Billiard` and `StarBilliard` are near-identical (`Cn_symmetry(n)` + `arc`/`wall1`/`wall0` pattern differing only in the coefficient vector and `n`); consolidating into one parametric constructor (or having `C3Billiard(a) = StarBilliard`-style(3, a, ...)) is a real, low-risk simplification opportunity now that both exist side-by-side.

### Direct answer to "which billiards' symmetries don't actually work?"
- **`LimaconBilliard`** — completely broken, `full_boundary` crashes outright (new finding).
- **`MushroomBilliard`** — completely broken, `full_boundary` silently returns a wrong, non-closed boundary even under correct default usage (new finding, and an algorithmic gap in `full_boundary` itself, not billiard-specific).
- **`C3Billiard`/`StarBilliard`** — `full_boundary` (used by BIM solvers) works correctly; only `pb_sectors`/`pb_coords` (Husimi-plotting path) crash, **confirming bg-02's tracked finding is still open and unregressed**, and now pinpointed to a different code path than `full_boundary`.
- **`StarBilliard`** additionally has a **new, confirmed** off-center construction vulnerability (no guard, silently wrong geometry).
- **Circle/Ellipse/Rectangle/Prosen/Stadium** (D2) — genuinely work correctly, runtime-verified, including the previously-untested live `CircleWedge` path.
- **Annular/Polar/Polygon/Triangle** — trivially "work" only because no symmetry folding is attempted (Annular's is a real under-registration, item 2; Polar/Polygon/Triangle's triviality is either correct-by-construction or, for Triangle's isosceles case, a missed opportunity, not a bug).
